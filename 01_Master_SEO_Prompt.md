AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator – Master Prompt

1. Project Overview

This project develops a reusable AI-powered prompt system that generates SEO-optimized blog posts and content clusters for business websites.

The system is demonstrated using Crumbz Cafe as the sample business.

2. Master Prompt

Role:

Act as an expert SEO content strategist, keyword researcher, and professional blog writer.

Objective:

Generate a complete SEO content strategy and blog content pack for a business website based on the business details and target audience provided.

Input Details

Business Name: Crumbz Cafe

Business Type: Cafe / Food and Beverage

Target Location: Anekal, Bengaluru, Karnataka

Target Audience: Students, working professionals, families, and cafe visitors

Website Type: Business Website

Main Topic: Cafe experiences, food, coffee, and hangouts

Language: English

Writing Style: Simple, engaging, informative, and professional

3. Required Output

A. Keyword Research and Search Intent

Generate relevant primary and secondary keywords.

Identify long-tail keywords.

Classify keywords by informational, commercial, and transactional search intent.

Suggest local SEO keywords.

B. Pillar Blog

Generate one detailed SEO-optimized pillar blog.

Requirements:

SEO-friendly title.

One H1 heading.

Proper H2 and H3 structure.

Engaging introduction.

Informative body content.

Relevant keyword placement.

Natural conclusion.

Meta title and meta description.

Suggested URL slug.

C. Supporting Blog Articles

Generate five supporting blog topics related to the pillar topic.

For each article, provide:

SEO-friendly title.

Primary keyword.

Secondary keywords.

Search intent.

Suggested headings.

Short article introduction.

D. Internal Linking Strategy

Connect supporting blogs to the pillar blog.

Suggest relevant anchor text.

Recommend contextual internal links.

Create a clear content cluster structure.

E. Local SEO Optimization

Include relevant city and service-based keywords.

Suggest location-specific blog topics.

Recommend natural local keyword placement.

Avoid unsupported claims about the business.

F. SEO Quality Checklist

Check that the generated content:

Matches the search intent.

Uses keywords naturally.

Has a clear heading hierarchy.

Is readable and informative.

Includes appropriate internal linking.

Avoids keyword stuffing.

Is original and business-focused.

4. Important Instructions

Generate useful content for real website visitors, not just search engines.

Avoid keyword stuffing and repetitive sentences.

Do not invent business details, prices, services, customer reviews, or offers.

Clearly identify information that needs verification.

Keep all articles relevant to the business and target audience.

Make the prompt reusable for other businesses by allowing users to change the input details.

5. Expected Result

Generate a complete AI-powered SEO Blog Pack containing:

Keyword research and search intent mapping.

One pillar blog article.

Five supporting blog topics.

Internal linking suggestions.

Local SEO recommendations.

SEO quality checklist.

End of Master Prompt
