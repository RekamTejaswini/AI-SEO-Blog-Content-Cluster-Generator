Reusable Prompt Demonstration

Purpose

This document demonstrates how the AI SEO Blog and Content Cluster Generator can be adapted for a different type of business by changing the input details in the master prompt.

Sample Business

Business Name: StyleHub Clothing Store
Business Type: Clothing and Fashion Store
Location: Anekal, Bengaluru
Target Audience: Local residents, students, and young adults

Note: StyleHub is a fictional example business created for demonstration purposes.

Updated Prompt Inputs

The following details can be entered into the reusable master prompt:

Business Name: StyleHub Clothing Store

Business Type: Clothing and Fashion Store

Location: Anekal, Bengaluru

Target Audience: Students, young adults, and local shoppers

Main Goal: Improve local search visibility and attract potential customers

Content Focus: Everyday clothing, casual wear, fashion tips, and shopping guides

Sample Keyword Suggestions

Primary Keyword: Clothing store in Anekal

Secondary Keywords:

Affordable clothing store in Anekal

Casual wear in Anekal

Fashion store near Anekal

Long-Tail Keywords:

Where to buy affordable casual clothes in Anekal

Everyday clothing ideas for college students

Tips for choosing comfortable everyday outfits

These are sample keyword ideas. Search volume and keyword difficulty have not been verified.

Search Intent

Informational: People looking for clothing tips and outfit ideas.

Commercial: People comparing clothing styles and prices.

Local: People searching for clothing stores in or near Anekal.

Sample Pillar Blog Topic

How to Choose Everyday Clothing That Matches Your Style

This article can cover clothing comfort, personal style, outfit combinations, and tips for choosing everyday wear.

Supporting Blog Topics

Sample SEO Metadata

Meta Description: Explore everyday clothing ideas, casual fashion tips, and simple ways to choose outfits that match your personal style.

Internal Linking Examples

Link the article about college outfits to the pillar blog.

Link the casual wear article to the everyday wardrobe guide.

Link the affordable fashion article to the clothing selection guide.

Expected Outcome

This demonstration shows how the same reusable SEO prompt structure can be adapted to another business category by changing the business information, target audience, location, and content focus.

The keyword ideas and article topics above are illustrative examples, not verified SEO research results.
